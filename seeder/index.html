Sound familiar?

The work is real. The presentation isn’t keeping up.

Funders, volunteers and partners often meet your organization online first. If what they see doesn’t match the work, it costs you.

  • Grant applications fall short

    Strong programs, but materials and presentation that don’t make the case.

  • Mixed messages

    Your story changes depending on who’s telling it.

  • Admin is improvised

    Records, reports and processes that make funders nervous.

  • Support is hard to attract

    Volunteers and donors aren’t finding you, or aren’t staying.
