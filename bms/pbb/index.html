Questions

Frequently asked

What happens after I submit the form? +
Your answers are sent instantly to our team at Build My Start-up and to your own inbox. A strategist reviews your brief within 1 to 2 business days and reaches out to confirm scope, timeline, and next steps.
Do I need to have a business already? +
No. This works whether you are an established entrepreneur with multiple ventures, a founder building your first company, a coach or consultant, a speaker, or an investor. If you have expertise and a story, this is for you.
What if I do not have professional photos yet? +
No problem. There is a question in the form about your photography situation. If you need a shoot, we can guide you on that as part of the onboarding process.
How long does it take to build my page? +
Timelines vary based on the scope of your project. Our team will give you a clear timeline once they have reviewed your brief. The questionnaire itself takes 10 to 15 minutes to complete.
Who owns the content and the page? +
You do. Everything we create for you belongs to you, including the copy, the design assets, and the code. Build My Start-up never retains ownership of client deliverables.
